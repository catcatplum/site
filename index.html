PlumFM

Your scrobbles, at home on your iPhone.

I made PlumFM because I love beautiful iOS apps. I also love music, and I love Last.fm.

I've been scrobbling to Last.fm since 2007. Along the way it became my personal music diary: my scrobble history is a record of the different chapters of my life, and the music that went with each one. Just how it should be.

But that diary always lived on the web. It never had a home on my most personal device, my iPhone. PlumFM is that home.

Roman

What's in it

  • Your recent scrobbles, top charts, loved tracks and friends
  • Your whole scrobble history, synced to your iPhone, so Insights can look back through all of it: streaks, milestones, Year in Review and forgotten favorites
  • Artist, album and track pages with your own history next to Last.fm's global numbers
  • Scrobbling for songs you've played in Apple Music
